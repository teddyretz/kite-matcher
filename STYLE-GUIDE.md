# FindMyKite Style & Design Guide

## Visual System — Daylight Outdoor-Utility

FindMyKite is a gear tool built to be read in bright sun at the beach or in a shop — not a dark-mode SaaS dashboard.

- **Palette**:
  - Background (`surface`): `#F6F5F0` — warm sailcloth off-white.
  - Panels & cards (`card`): `#FFFFFF` with crisp `1px` borders (`#D4D1C7`).
  - Ink (`slate`): `#17191C` high-contrast charcoal; secondary text `#57544D`.
  - Primary utilitarian accent (`ocean`): `#0E5A8A` deep maritime blue.
  - Signal / tradeoff accent (`sand`): `#B44B1E` muted terracotta.
- **No AI visual clichés**: Zero neon glows, zero radial blur orbs, zero dark glassmorphism (`backdrop-blur`), zero decorative gradients.
- **Typography**:
  - UI & prose: `Inter` (`font-sans`).
  - Specs, wind ranges, strut counts, prices, sizes, discipline scores, and fit scores: `IBM Plex Mono` (`font-mono tabular-nums`).
- **Controls**:
  - Discrete kiter choices (discipline, board type, skill level, bar system, construction, bar feel) use segmented buttons/toggles.
  - Sliders are reserved strictly for true continuums (wind speed in knots, budget in USD, optional rider weight in kg).

## Writing Voice (Ted's Guide)

Write like a smart friend thinking out loud, not a pundit or a brochure.

- **Direct and blunt**: Keep verdicts specific and anchored to linked video or owner sources. Cut launch-copy fluff.
- **Specificity over hype**: Not "amazing performance across all conditions" — say "holds to 35 kt and jumps high, but stalls mid-loop if you pull early."
- **Honest tradeoffs**: Surface real downsides alongside strengths. Every source is labeled by commercial tie (`independent`, `owner`, `retailer`, `brand`, `unverified`).
- **Banned AI words**: *tapestry*, *landscape* (as metaphor), *delve*, *underscore*, *pivotal*, *testament*, *vibrant*, *showcase*, *nuanced*, *foster*, *crucial*, *key* (as adjective), *elevate*, *unlock*.
- **Clean rhythm**: Short sentences when something lands. Don't announce what you're about to say — just say it.
